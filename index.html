Dear Intrepid Traveller

Northern Specials

The Samburu Special Five

Samburu National Reserve is renowned for five species endemic to Northern Kenya, rarely seen further south.

Grevy's Zebra Equus grevyi

The largest and most endangered zebra species — known for its narrower stripes, white underbelly, larger ears and more donkey-like build than other zebras.

[Photo — reticulated giraffe]
Reticulated Giraffe Giraffa camelopardalis reticulata

Distinguished by a striking coat of large, polygonal patches resembling a net — a pattern that helps it blend into the surrounding acacia trees.

[Photo — Beisa oryx]
Beisa Oryx Oryx beisa

A large antelope with long, straight, backward-curving horns, exceptionally well adapted to arid and semi-arid conditions — able to survive without water for long periods.

Gerenuk Litocranius walleri

Its name means "giraffe-necked" in Somali. The long neck and legs let it reach acacia leaves and branches other antelope can't access.

[Photo — Somali ostrich]
Somali Ostrich Struthio molybdophanes

Native to the Horn of Africa, identifiable by its distinctive blue-grey neck and legs — well adapted to this arid landscape.

Sustainability

Community & Wildlife Conservation

Soroi Samburu Lodge is an eco-friendly lodge built with local materials, running on solar power with a backup generator, and does not allow single-use plastics. Community bed-night fees are paid monthly to the local community.

Through our partnership with Community & Wildlife Conservation (CWC) — a grassroots initiative founded in 2020 — your stay directly supports education, healthcare, hygiene support, women's and youth empowerment, and anti-poaching efforts across the communities surrounding Soroi properties.

Safety

Your Wellbeing

In Case of Fire
  • Exit the room immediately and move to a clear area — do not stop for belongings.
  • Report any fire to a staff member immediately.
  • Proceed to the assembly point at the Lodge entrance for a head count.
  • Do not re-enter until management has declared it safe.
Night Safety

Soroi Samburu Lodge is unfenced — wild animals surround you. Guards protect the lodge 24/7, and an askari will accompany you around the lodge at night. Please do not walk alone at night — call reception for an escort.

Emergencies & Medical

For anything unexpected, contact Reception, Security, or the Lodge Manager via the intercom. The lodge has a resident nurse and can arrange transport to the nearest medical facility if needed.
